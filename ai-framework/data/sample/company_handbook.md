# Nimbus Analytics Employee Handbook (Sample)

This is a fictional handbook used to demonstrate retrieval-augmented generation.

## Paid Time Off

New employees receive 15 days of paid time off (PTO) per year. After three years of service, PTO increases to 20 days per year. Unused PTO rolls over up to a maximum of 5 days into the next calendar year.

PTO requests of three or more consecutive days must be submitted in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance.

## Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Fully remote arrangements require approval from both the employee's manager and the VP of People.

The company provides a one-time home office stipend of $500 for new hires, reimbursed through the expense system within the first 90 days.

## Learning and Development

Each employee has an annual learning budget of $1,500 for courses, certifications, and conferences. Requests above $500 require manager approval before purchase.

## Expenses

Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase date. Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to $75 per day.
