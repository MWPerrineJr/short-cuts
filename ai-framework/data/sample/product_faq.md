# Nimbus Analytics Product FAQ (Sample)

This is a fictional FAQ used to demonstrate retrieval-augmented generation.

## What is Nimbus Analytics?

Nimbus Analytics is a cloud platform for building dashboards and running forecasts on business data. It connects to Postgres, Snowflake, BigQuery, and CSV uploads.

## What plans are available?

The Starter plan costs $29 per user per month and includes 5 dashboards. The Pro plan costs $79 per user per month and includes unlimited dashboards, scheduled reports, and forecasting. Enterprise pricing is custom and adds single sign-on and a dedicated support engineer.

## What does error code NX-404 mean?

Error NX-404 means a data source connection was revoked or its credentials expired. Reconnect the data source under Settings > Connections to resolve it.

## What does error code NX-500 mean?

Error NX-500 means a scheduled report failed because the query exceeded the 10-minute timeout. Simplify the query or add filters to reduce the data scanned.

## How long is data retained?

Query results are cached for 24 hours. Audit logs are retained for 1 year on Pro and 7 years on Enterprise.

## Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every plan has a 14-day free trial, and no credit card is required to start.
